# Initial Concept
A Python-based financial data agent designed to collect, cache, analyze, and query stock market data from multiple financial data sources, initially focusing on the Taiwan stock market using data from Yahoo Finance.

## Target Audience
- **Individual Investors:** Retail investors looking for personal portfolio insights, stock screening, and easy access to financial data.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Natural Language Querying:** Allowing users to ask complex financial questions in plain English.
- **Local Data Ownership:** Caching data locally in SQLite/PostgreSQL for fast, offline access and privacy.
- **Extensibility:** A modular architecture that easily supports new data sources and markets.

## Key Features (Initial Release)
- **LLM-powered Analysis:** Integrating LLMs to provide insights and summaries of the financial data.
- **Advanced Stock Screening:** Implementing complex filters like "dividend yield > 5%" or "increased > 5% today".
- **Robust Data Synchronization:** Ensuring reliable, scheduled, or on-demand data pulls from Yahoo Finance.

## Future Expansion
- **Additional Markets:** Expanding beyond the Taiwan stock market to US, European, or other Asian markets.
